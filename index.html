How Aerio works

Three steps. Minutes, not weeks.

01 / Launch

Stand a project up in minutes.

Describe the work in plain prose. AI extracts the tasks. Invite anyone — inside the org or out — by email. They click a magic link and they're in. No accounts, no friction, no welcome flow.

02 / Surface

Every file and link, search-ready.

Pull in PDFs, decks, spreadsheets, Google Docs, Dropbox, OneDrive — any source. Each is auto-summarized at upload, so the next question you ask is already prepared for.

03 / Synthesize

Ask anything. Get cited answers.

Aim Analyze at any project, pick the data, get a grounded answer. Every claim traces back to its source — server-enforced. Stack analyses on top of each other to go deeper, faster.
